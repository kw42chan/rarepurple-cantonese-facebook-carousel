# Carousel structure

Default to 7 pages; use 6 when the source is compact.

1. Cover hook: one office complaint and immediate visual tension.
2. Pain point: show repetitive or confusing work and its cost.
3. Consequence: show the awkward meeting, deadline, error, or loss of confidence.
4. AI solution: input → processing → useful output in three steps.
5. Before/after: compare the old state with the structured result.
6. Emotional payoff: show control, confidence, or time back.
7. CTA: show the relevant AI 拍拍機 card and exact CTA lines.

Every page needs one short headline, one visual idea, and only a few supporting labels. The story should remain understandable from headlines alone.
