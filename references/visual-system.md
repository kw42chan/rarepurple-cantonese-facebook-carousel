# RarePurple visual system

## Direction

Premium, cinematic, editorial 3D advertising for social media. The visual should feel like a polished AI product campaign: dramatic but clean, expressive but not childish.

## Palette

- 70% deep royal purple / dark violet background.
- 20% warm white or cream information surfaces and type.
- 8% metallic warm gold accents.
- 2% functional accents such as amber, green, or red status markers.

Use subtle mottled texture, paper grain, restrained gold dust or brush texture, soft vignette, and cinematic rim lighting. Avoid flat pale purple, pastel palettes, neon cyberpunk, or generic template styling.

## Typography and layout

- Traditional Chinese: extra-heavy Gothic/block style, white or warm cream with gold emphasis.
- Latin: heavy condensed sans serif, such as an Anton/Impact-like treatment.
- Keep text very large, concise, high contrast, and readable on a phone.
- Use about 5% safe margins, one dominant idea per page, and a strong hierarchy.
- Use badges, cards, arrows, checklists, comparison panels, and CTA strips as needed.

## Generation guardrails

Keep the same palette, lighting logic, rendering quality, and headline treatment on every page. Render exact Chinese text in a later design/layout pass when the image model cannot reliably typeset it. Do not add logos, claims, interface elements, or decorative objects not requested by the page prompt.
