# Optional recurring character system

Use the recurring protagonist for workplace story pages where a character helps explain the copy. Product heroes, benefit panels, lifestyle photo collages, and product-only CTA pages may omit the character. Follow the image-treatment and palette rules in [visual-system.md](visual-system.md); do not force cartoon rendering onto product photography or lifestyle photos.

When the recurring character is used, retain this identity across those pages: East Asian Hong Kong office worker, late 20s to early 30s, dark-brown messy bun with loose strands, expressive large eyes, gold hoop earrings, dark charcoal blazer, light cream top, office lanyard, and subtle watch.

Render premium expressive 3D artwork with soft skin shading, convincing materials, and diffuse studio or office lighting. Keep the character separate from the lavender background and clear of text. Avoid childish cartoon proportions, toy-like plastic, exaggerated gloss, and dramatic rim lighting.

Story states: problem = stressed or embarrassed; solution = focused and curious; result = relaxed, confident, and happy. Repeat the identity block in prompts for pages containing this character so appearance stays consistent while expression and pose change. Use supplied lifestyle photographs as photographs, without converting their subjects into the recurring character.
