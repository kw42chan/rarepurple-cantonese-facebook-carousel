# rarepurple-cantonese-facebook-carousel

Reusable Agent Skill for creating polished Traditional Chinese Cantonese Facebook/Instagram carousels for AI 拍拍機 and Hong Kong office audiences.

## What it produces

- A carousel at the supplied page count and storyboard; otherwise a 6–7 page narrative carousel.
- Page-by-page image prompts with exact artwork copy.
- Page-appropriate product cutouts, white-bordered lifestyle photos, or an optional recurring premium 3D East Asian female office-worker character.
- A soft lavender-grey, deep-purple, warm-gold, and ivory visual direction with oversized upright sans-serif headlines and gold-outlined ivory panels.
- A Facebook-optimized Cantonese caption with hook, story, AI solution, transformation, CTA, hashtags, and a comment-driving question.

The current [visual system](references/visual-system.md) supersedes the former dark-purple cinematic theme. Supplied references and explicit user design requests take precedence. Preserve supplied wording, punctuation, English casing, page order, CTA, and hashtags.

## Three-page daily-learning preset

Use this reusable variant when recreating or extending the approved daily AI learning carousel. Other carousel briefs can use the general page layouts.

| Page | Design |
| --- | --- |
| 1 — Hook | Plain pale lavender background, with no character, cube box, desk, laptop, or scene props. Upright extra-heavy purple/gold headline, left aligned, occupying approximately 70% of page height. Small centred brand badge above; divider and page number below. |
| 2 — Product and learning system | Recurring 3D office worker replaces the cube-box hero in the main illustration area. Keep the headline and ivory gold-bordered product/system panels clear of the character. |
| 3 — Product, screenshot and CTA | Relevant product image plus a genuine supplied or captured self-study-base screenshot and the requested CTA. Continue the lavender background, upright purple/gold typography, and rounded purple CTA treatment with optional fine gold outline. |

Headline occupancy refers to the text block's vertical extent relative to the full canvas. Size and alignment are independent: retain a requested 70% or 50% layout when changing alignment, without moving or resizing the badge and footer.

The third-page preset does not fix headline occupancy, alignment, or the product/screenshot's left-right placement. Use the current brief to determine those details. Campaign-specific headline copy is supplied by the user rather than hard-coded into the skill.

Use a real screenshot; never invent a website interface. If it is unavailable, identify the page as a draft and disclose the missing asset.
